Command Syntax Example Behavior
Roll & Move r <player> <d1> <d2> [speed] r m 4 5 bus Moves token 9 steps. Speed accepts 1-3, bus, mr_m.
Buy Property b <player> <prop> [cashPaid] b m vadodara 50 Buys property. Remainder automatically becomes bank loan.
Pay Rent p <payer> <receiver> <amt> p m d 150 Transfers rent cash directly between players.
Build House/Hotel h <player> <prop> <lvl> h m agra 5 Upgrades deed: 1-4 (Houses), 2.5 (Motel), 5 (Hotel).
Collect Bank c <player> <amt> c m 200 Awards money from Bank (Salary / GO / Cards).
Bank Loan l <player> <amt> l m 500 Adds cash to player and records equal loan debt.
Repay Bank rl <player> <amt> rl m 500 Clears cash and wipes out outstanding bank debt.
Player Borrow pl <borrower> <lender> <amt> pl m d 300 Transfers cash from lender to borrower + records debt.
Repay Player rpl <borrower> <lender> <amt> rpl m d 300 Returns cash to lender and clears borrower debt.
Tax / Fee fee <player> <amt> fee m 100 Deducts cash to Bank for jail, luxury tax, or card fines.
Smart Abbreviation Matching:

You do not need to type exact full names. The compiler resolves the shortest unique prefix for players (m → Mitansh, d → Dhyana) and properties (vado → Vadodara, csm → Station 4 (CSMT), elec → Electric Company).
